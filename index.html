ML6CMG Chord & Lyrics Builder — Help

Lyrics

  • Enter your lyrics one line at a time.
  • Press Enter after each lyric line.
  • The app creates a dedicated chord line above each lyric line.
  • There is deliberately space before the first lyric word so chords can be placed there.

Placing chords

  • Use the chord bank on the right-hand side.
  • The chord bank stays visible while you work on the lyrics.
  • Tap a chord to select it. The button lights up.
  • Then tap anywhere on the chord line to place it.
  • You can also drag and drop a chord onto the exact position you want.
  • Chords are stored using their precise horizontal position rather than snapping to the start of a lyric line.
  • This means you can position a chord over the exact syllable being sung.

Moving chords

  • Drag an unlocked chord left or right.
  • The chord remains at the position where you release it.
  • It does not automatically snap back to the start of the line.
  • Triple-click/tap a chord to lock it.
  • Triple-click/tap the locked chord again to unlock it.

Chord variations

  • Hold a chord for 4 seconds.
  • A variation menu will appear with options such as C7, C9, Cmaj7, Csus2 and Csus4.
  • The 4-second delay gives you enough time to drag a chord without accidentally opening the variation menu.

Chord progression

  • Each progression chord has a beat count.
  • Use + to add beats.
  • Use − to remove beats.
  • The count will not go below 1 beat.

Capo

  • Select the capo fret in Song Information.
  • The main chord shown is the chord you physically play.
  • The smaller chord in brackets above it is the actual sounding/uncapo chord.
  • For example, Capo 3 + E shape produces a sounding G.
  • Capo 0 disables the secondary chord display.

Saving

  • The app automatically saves your work locally as you make changes.
  • Use Save / Update Song for a deliberate save.
  • Use Previous Songs to retrieve saved songs.
  • Use Save & Start New Song when beginning another song.
  • Saved songs are stored on the device/browser. Clearing browser data can remove them.

Tablet / Mobile

  • The app is designed primarily for tablet use.
  • On a phone, landscape mode is recommended when adding chords.
  • The chord bank remains on screen beside the lyrics while working.

Recording

  • You can record using your device microphone.
  • You can also select an existing audio file.
  • Microphone permission may be required.
