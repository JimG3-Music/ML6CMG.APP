ML6CMG Chord & Lyrics Builder — Help

📱 Recommended device

This app is designed primarily for use on a tablet such as an iPad. On a mobile phone, landscape mode is recommended while working with lyrics and chords.

📝 Entering lyrics

  • Enter your lyrics one line at a time.
  • Press Enter after each lyric line.
  • The app creates a dedicated chord area above each lyric line.
  • There is intentional space before the first lyric word so a chord can be placed there.

🎸 Adding chords

  • The chord bank remains visible on the right while working with lyrics.
  • Tap a chord to select it. The button lights up so you know it is selected.
  • You can then tap a position in the chord area above the lyrics.
  • You can also drag and drop a chord onto the desired position.
  • Chords can be positioned independently of the beginning of the lyric line.

↔️ Moving chords

  • Placed chords can be dragged left and right.
  • The chord stays at the exact horizontal position where you place it.
  • This allows chords to sit above the exact word or even the appropriate syllable.

🔒 Locking chords

  • Triple-click a placed chord to lock it.
  • A locked chord cannot accidentally be moved.
  • Triple-click the locked chord again to unlock it.

🎼 Chord variations

  • Long-press a chord for approximately 4 seconds.
  • A variation menu will appear with options such as C7, C9, Cmaj7, Csus2 and Csus4.
  • The 4-second delay helps prevent the variation menu appearing accidentally while dragging.

⏱️ Chord beats

  • Each chord in the progression has a beat count.
  • Press + to increase the count.
  • Press − to decrease the count.
  • The minimum is 1 beat.

🎸 Intro

Use Add Intro to create a dedicated intro section for chord arrangements before the lyrics begin.

💾 Saving songs

  • The app autosaves your current work on this device as you make changes.
  • Use Save Song to create/update a named saved song.
  • Use My Saved Songs to access previous songs.
  • Choose Edit to bring a previous song back into the editor.
  • Choose Delete to remove a saved song.
  • Start New Song creates a fresh workspace without deleting your previous songs.

⚠️ Important

Saved songs are stored locally in this browser/device. For important work, always keep your own backup copies. Do not rely solely on this app for permanent storage.
