ML6CMG Chord & Lyrics Builder — HELP

This app is designed to help you build a playable chord and lyrics sheet.

1. Entering lyrics

  • Enter your lyrics one line at a time.
  • Press Enter after each lyric line.
  • The app preserves the individual lines so you can work through the song.
  • A space is automatically provided before the first lyric word so chords can be positioned above the beginning of the line.

2. Selecting chords

  • Choose chords from the Circle of Fifths or the available chord lists.
  • Your selected chords appear in the chord bank on the right while you work.
  • Tap a chord to select it. The button lights up so you know which chord is selected.

3. Adding chords to lyrics

  • Tap the exact point on the chord line where you want the chord.
  • You can also drag a chord from the available chord bank onto the lyrics.
  • Chords are positioned by pixel position rather than being forced to the beginning of a word.
  • This means you can position a chord over the exact syllable being sung.

4. Moving chords

  • Drag an unlocked chord left or right.
  • The chord remains at the exact horizontal position where you release it.
  • It does not automatically snap back to the beginning of the line.

5. Locking chords

  • Triple-click a chord to lock it.
  • A locked chord cannot accidentally be moved.
  • Triple-click it again to unlock it.

6. Chord variations

  • Long-press a chord for approximately two seconds.
  • A menu will appear with variations such as C7, C9, Cmaj7, Csus2, Csus4 and others.

7. Chord progression beats

  • Each chord in the progression starts at 4 beats.
  • Use − to reduce the number of beats.
  • Use + to increase the number of beats.
  • The available range is 1–16 beats.

8. Intro

  • Use Add Intro Section to create a separate chord area for your introduction.
  • Add the chords used during the intro before the lyrics begin.

9. Saving

  • The app automatically saves your work on this device as you make changes.
  • You can also use Save / Update manually.
  • Load Saved Song retrieves the locally saved version.
  • Use the download buttons to create text copies of your work.

10. Recording

  • Use Start Recording to record through your device microphone.
  • You can also choose an existing audio file.
  • Allow microphone access when your device asks.

11. Important backup advice

Please save important lyrics, chords, recordings and other files to your own device. Do not rely solely on this app for permanent storage or posterity.
