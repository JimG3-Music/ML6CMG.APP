ML6CMG Chord & Lyrics Builder — Help

1. Enter your lyrics

  • Enter your lyrics one line at a time.
  • Press Build / Update Lyrics.
  • The app creates space above each lyric line for chords.

2. Choose a chord

  • The chord panel remains visible beside your lyrics.
  • Tap a chord and it will light up.
  • The selected chord is also shown at the top of the panel.

3. Place a chord

  • Tap anywhere along the chord line above the lyrics.
  • The chord will appear exactly at that position.
  • You are not restricted to the beginning of a word.
  • This allows you to position a chord directly over the syllable where it is actually sung.

4. Move a chord

  • Drag the placed chord left or right.
  • The position is stored in pixels, so it does not automatically snap back to the start of the line.

5. Lock a chord

  • Triple-click a placed chord to lock it.
  • A locked chord cannot accidentally be moved.
  • Triple-click it again to unlock it.

6. Chord variations

  • Press and hold a chord for approximately two seconds.
  • A variation menu will appear.
  • Examples include C7, C9, Cmaj7, Cadd9, Csus2, Csus4, Cdim and Caug.

7. Beat counts

  • Each progression chord has a − and + button.
  • Use these to reduce or increase the number of beats.

8. Lock & Store Line

  • Once you have finished a lyric line, use Lock & Store Line.
  • The completed line can be collapsed to reduce clutter.
  • The document preview continues updating.

9. Automatic saving

  • The app automatically saves your current work to the device while you work.
  • You can also manually use Save / Update.
  • For important work, always save/export a copy yourself.

10. Intro Chords

  • Use Add Intro Chords to create a separate area for the instrumental introduction before the lyrics.
