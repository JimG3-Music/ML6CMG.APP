ML6CMG Chord & Lyrics Builder — Help

This app is designed to help you build a playable lyrics and chords sheet.

  • Enter your lyrics one line at a time. Leave each lyric line on its own line.
  • The app automatically provides a chord line above each lyric line, including space before the first word so a chord can be positioned at the beginning.
  • The Selected Chords bank stays visible on the right while you work.
  • Drag a chord from the right-hand chord bank onto the chord line.
  • You can also tap a chord to select it, then tap anywhere on the chord line.
  • Chords are stored using their exact horizontal position. They do not automatically snap back to the beginning of the lyric line.
  • Drag a placed chord left or right to position it over the exact word or syllable you want.
  • Triple-click/tap a placed chord to lock it. Triple-click/tap it again to unlock it.
  • Long-press a chord in the chord bank for additional chord variations such as C7, C9, Cmaj7, Csus2, Csus4 and others.
  • The lyrics are arranged in groups of four lines to make chord entry quicker.
  • Once you finish a group of four lines you can use Lock & Store to reduce clutter while continuing with the remainder of the song.
  • The preview at the bottom continues updating as you work.
  • The chord progression beat counter can be increased or decreased using the + and − buttons.
  • Use Add Intro if you want chords before the first lyric.
  • Your work is automatically saved to this device as you make changes.
  • Use Save / Update if you want to deliberately create an additional saved copy.

Important: Automatic saving uses this browser/device's local storage. Clearing browser data, changing devices or other browser events can remove it.
