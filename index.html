ML6CMG Chord & Lyrics Builder — Help

  • 1. Enter your lyrics
    Enter your lyrics one line at a time. You can paste the complete lyrics into the box, with each lyric line separated by a line break.
  • 2. Select your chords
    Choose chords from the key section, extended chords, Circle of Fifths, or your Selected Chords.
  • 3. Place a chord
    Select a chord from the floating Selected Chords area, then tap anywhere on the dark chord-placement line above the lyrics. The chord will appear at that exact horizontal position.
  • 4. Syllable placement
    You are not restricted to the beginning of a word. Place the chord directly above the syllable where the chord changes.
  • 5. Move a chord
    Drag an unlocked chord left or right. It uses its actual position rather than snapping to a word. Small movements are retained.
  • 6. Lock a chord
    Triple-click a chord to lock it. A locked chord cannot accidentally be dragged. Triple-click it again to unlock it.
  • 7. Lock & Store a line
    When you have finished a lyric line, press Lock & Store Line. The line collapses to reduce clutter while remaining part of your song and the preview continues to update.
  • 8. Chord Intro
    Use + Chord Intro to create an instrumental chord introduction before the lyrics.
  • 9. Floating Selected Chords
    The Selected Chords tray stays at the top of the working area while you scroll through your song, so you don't have to keep scrolling back up.
  • 10. Auto-save
    Your work is automatically saved to this device as you make changes. You should still export/save your important work separately.
  • 11. Preview
    The document preview at the bottom updates as you work so you can see the formatted result.
  • 12. Recording
    You can record using your device microphone or select an existing audio file.
