ML6CMG Chord & Lyrics Builder — HELP

📱 Best device / orientation
The app is designed to be used on a tablet or in landscape mode on a mobile device for the easiest chord placement and editing experience.
🎤 Entering lyrics
Enter your lyrics one line at a time. Each line is kept separate so the app can provide a dedicated chord-placement area above it. The app creates the necessary space above the lyrics so chords can be positioned over the exact word or syllable being sung.
🎵 Selecting chords
Your available chords appear in the chord bank on the right-hand side while you work on your lyrics. You do not need to scroll back to the top to find another chord.
↔️ Moving chords
Drag a placed chord left or right. The chord position is stored using a precise pixel position rather than simply snapping it back to the beginning of a word. This allows you to place a chord over a particular syllable or even between letters if required.
🖱️ Drag & drop
Drag a chord from the right-hand chord bank onto the chord line above the lyrics. You can then move the chord horizontally until it sits exactly where you want it.
👆 Tap placement
Tap a chord in the bank. It will light up to show that it is selected. Then tap anywhere along the chord-placement line. The chord will appear at that exact position.
🔒 Lock a chord
Triple-click or triple-tap a placed chord to lock it. A locked chord cannot accidentally be moved. Triple-click or triple-tap it again to unlock it.
⏱️ Chord variations
Press and hold a chord for approximately 4 seconds. A variation menu will appear with options such as: C7 • C9 • Cmaj7 • Cadd9 • Csus2 • Csus4 • Cdim • Caug The 4-second delay allows enough time to drag chords without accidentally opening the variation menu.
➕➖ Chord beats
In Chord Progression, use the + button to add beats and the − button to remove beats. The count can be reduced down to one beat.
🔒 Store / collapse completed lines
Once you have completed a lyric line, use the Lock / Store button. The line can be collapsed to reduce the amount of information on screen while you work on the remainder of the song. The document preview continues updating at the bottom.
💾 Automatic saving
The app automatically saves your work to the device's browser storage as you make changes. This is designed to reduce the chance of losing work if you leave the page. However, automatic browser storage should not be treated as your only backup.
💿 Export your work
Use the Save buttons in Document Preview to create text files containing:
• Chords + Lyrics
• Chords + Strumming
• Lyrics Only
🎙️ Recording
The recording section allows you to record using your device microphone or select an existing audio file. Microphone permission may be required.
🎼 Circle of Fifths
Select a key and use the Circle of Fifths to quickly add relevant chords. Tap a chord to add it to your available chord bank.
⚠️ IMPORTANT BACKUP NOTICE
Please ensure you save any relevant files to your own device and do not solely rely on this app for posterity.
