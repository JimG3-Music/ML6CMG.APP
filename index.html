ML6CMG Chord & Lyrics Builder — HELP

1. Enter your lyrics

Enter or paste your lyrics into the main lyrics box. For best results, put one lyric line on each line.

2. Build the lyrics

Press Build / Update Lyrics. The app will turn your lyrics into the chord-placement workspace. Four lyric lines are displayed together so you can work faster.

3. Add chords

Choose chords from the Circle of Fifths, the relevant chord list, or the extended chord list. They will appear in your Selected Chords tray.

4. Place a chord

  • Drag a chord from the Selected Chords tray onto the required lyric line.
  • You can also tap a chord and then tap the lyric position where you want it.
  • There is deliberate space before the first lyric word so a chord can be placed at the very beginning of a line.

5. Make the chord syllable-correct

After placing a chord, simply drag it left or right. The chord can be positioned independently of the lyric words. This lets you line it up with the exact word or syllable where the chord change happens.

6. Lock a chord

Triple-click a placed chord to lock it. A locked chord cannot accidentally be dragged.

Triple-click the locked chord again to unlock it. You can then move it again.

7. Intro chords

Use Add Intro Chords to create a chord-only intro before the lyrics. You can enter chords such as:

C — G — Am — F

8. Four-line editing

The editor shows four lyric lines at the same time. Use the Previous and Next buttons to move through the song. Your entire song remains one continuous set of lyrics.

9. Automatic saving

The app automatically saves your work to the device while you work. It also attempts to save when you leave or close the page. Use Save / Update as an additional manual backup.

10. Recording

You can record using your device microphone or select an existing audio file.

11. Strumming

Enable strumming and tap the boxes to cycle between downstroke, upstroke and blank.

12. Important backup reminder

The automatic save is stored in the browser/device. Clearing browser data, changing devices or other browser actions may remove locally stored information.

Please ensure you save any relevant files to your own device and do not solely rely on this app for posterity.
