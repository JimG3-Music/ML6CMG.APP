ML6CMG Chord & Lyrics Builder — HELP

  • Enter lyrics: Enter your lyrics one line at a time and press Return after each line. The app creates the chord space above each lyric line automatically.
  • Space before the first word: The chord area always provides room before the first sung word, so an opening chord can be positioned there.
  • Place a chord: On iPad/iPhone, tap a chord in the chord bank to select it, then tap anywhere along the chord line. The chord will appear at that exact position.
  • Drag a chord: You can drag a chord left or right until it sits directly above the correct word or syllable.
  • Syllable placement: Chords are stored by their actual horizontal position. They do not automatically snap back to the beginning of a word or line.
  • Move a chord: Touch and drag the placed chord horizontally. Small movements are preserved.
  • Lock a chord: Triple-tap a placed chord to lock it. Triple-tap it again to unlock it. A locked chord cannot accidentally be moved.
  • Select a chord: Selected chords light up so you can see which chord you have chosen.
  • Chord variations: Long-press a chord for approximately two seconds. You can then choose variations such as C7, C9, Cmaj7, Cadd9, Csus2, Csus4, Cdim and Caug, with appropriate minor variations where available.
  • Chord progression: Use + and − beside each chord to increase or reduce the number of beats.
  • Intro: Use Add Intro to create a dedicated chord-only intro section before the lyrics.
  • Lock/store a lyric line: Once you have finished a lyric line, use Lock / Store Line. The line can be collapsed so there is less on-screen clutter while you work through the remaining lyrics. The document preview continues updating.
  • Automatic saving: The app automatically saves your work to the browser storage as you make changes and also attempts to save when you leave the page.
  • Mobile: On iPhone and iPad, the chord bank remains available without having to continually scroll back to the top. On smaller screens it becomes a fixed tray at the bottom.
  • Preview: The document preview at the bottom shows the formatted song as you build it.
  • Recording: You can record using the device microphone and attach an existing audio file.
  • Save your work: Use the Save / Update function and also save/export important song files to your own device.
