Please enter your lyrics one line at a time.

For example:
My shoes are new
I've got big feet
I walk them down the street

Press Enter after each line. The app will keep your lyrics together and will create the spaces above the lyrics where you can add chords later.

You can work through four lines at a time rather than having to edit one lyric line separately. Drag a chord onto the lyric, then drag the placed chord left or right until it sits directly above the syllable you sing.
